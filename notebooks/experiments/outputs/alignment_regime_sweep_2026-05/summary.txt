Methodology — side-experiment work feeding DECISIONS Decision 26
================================================================

Q64 IS-style 4-regime visual comparison (Addendum #54)
  Per-bp 4-regime cross-session pooled-heatmap grids, lightweight Option B
  (14 sampled SVGs for PS46+PS48 only). Predecessor visual stack to the
  Q65/Q66 PD-comparison work.

Q65 / Q66 / Q70 / Q71 PD-comparison 4-regime layout (Addenda #55/#56/#58/#59)
  Per-bin proportion-difference heatmaps across 4 alignment regimes,
  rendered for (animal, family, scope, trial_group) tuples.
    Q65: post-stroke bins only, PS46+PS48.
    Q66: pre-stroke bins added as null-reference rows.
    Q70: full 8-animal cohort, dropped tongue_spout_midpoint (redundant
         with tongue_eye_midpoint for tongue-only data); 6 family entries.
    Q71: whole_session scope added alongside tone_aligned (Q49 WS-bounded
         frame filter for whole_session cells).
  Cmap-direction bug caught + corrected at Q70+Q71 launch: cmap stops
  flipped to Q25 canonical [(0.0,"blue"),(0.5,grey),(1.0,"red")] so
  red=increased post-stroke, blue=decreased post-stroke.
  Total render: 192 PD-comparison SVGs + 14 Q64 IS-style SVGs = 206 SVGs
  under svg_comparison/<animal>/.

Q72 per-bin numeric aggregation (Decision 26 input)
  Within-bin pairwise Pearson r + JS divergence + diff-vs-baseline
  magnitude per (animal, bp, scope, trial_group, bin, regime). Two
  variants:
    - All-bins:    per_bin_numeric_comparison.csv (16,432 rows)
    - Pre-stroke:  per_bin_numeric_comparison_pre_stroke.csv (4,368 rows)
  Plus cross-animal aggregation + per-bin regime-winner summary CSVs
  for each variant.

Per-animal cohort-wide overview figures (Part A, 2026-05-21)
  Per-(animal, regime, bodypart) pairwise Pearson r + JS divergence
  pooled across ALL pre-stroke session pairs (no bin slicing). Matches
  the 2026-04-19 sweep's per-animal-aggregation semantics exactly so
  the 2026-04-19 baseline figures at ../alignment_sweep/00_metric_overview_*.png
  are direct comparators.
    - per_animal_metrics_pre_stroke.csv (416 rows)
    - per_animal_metrics_all_bins.csv (416 rows)
    - 4 PNGs at 00_metric_overview_{pearson,js_divergence}_{pre_stroke,all_bins}.png
  Histogram-edge logic ported from 2026-04-19 sweep cell 8 verbatim
  (pooled-data min/max + HEATMAP_MARGIN_PX=20; NOT YAML-config-derived;
  per Q13 audit-first ground rule).
